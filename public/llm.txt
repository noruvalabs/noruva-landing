# Noruva Labs

> Noruva Labs is a software company building applied AI products and custom agentic systems.

## About

Noruva Labs builds three AI assistants and provides custom agentic AI engineering services:

- **Interview Assistant** — Structured question sets, live notes and comparable candidate scorecards.
- **Immigration Assistant** — Route guidance, document checklists and deadline tracking per case.
- **Law Assistant** — Research, summaries and drafting with a citation behind every claim.
- **Agentic AI services** — Custom agents designed, built and integrated into your existing stack.

## Products

### Interview Assistant
Turns a job description into a structured interview loop: tailored questions, live note capture, rubric scoring and a candidate brief the panel can actually compare.

### Immigration Assistant
Walks an applicant or consultant through the right route, builds the document checklist, flags missing evidence, and keeps track of deadlines across a whole caseload.

### Law Assistant
Research, summarisation and drafting grounded in your own matter files and public sources — every answer carries a citation back to the paragraph it came from.
